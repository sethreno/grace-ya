# The Budding Fig Tree

## Verses
- Mathew 24:32-36
- Mark 13:28-33
- Luke 21:29-36

## Discussion Questions
- Thoughts, observations?
- What's Jesus referring to when he says "When you see all these things"?
- Who is Jesus referring to when he says "this generation" or "this people"?
- How can we be prepared without knowing the day or hour?
- How can we be prepared without becoming anxious, obsessive, or paralyzed?
- What are your thoughts on Jesus' statement: "Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away."
