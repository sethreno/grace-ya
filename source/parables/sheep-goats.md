# The Sheep & The Goats

## Verses
- Mathew 25:31-46

## Discussion Questions
- Thoughts, observations?
- Did the sheep or goats realize they were serving or ignoring Jesus?
- What does this say about calculated charity?
- Who are the least of these in our community?
- How does the distinction "the least of these *my brothers*" change or deepen the meaning of the text?
- Imagine Jesus were to evaluate your daily habits and priorities based on this parable, which areas do you feel sheep-like or goat-like?
- The goats are not condemned for an active malicious crime, but for what the failed to do. Does this challenge your view of sin and righteousness in your daily life?
