# The Two Debtors 

## Verses
- Luke 7:36-50
- Preached in ([Capernaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capernaum){:target="_blank"})

## Discussion Questions
- Is it surprising that Jesus is having dinner with one of the Pharisees?
- Is Jesus saying we must sin more so we can love Him more?
- Can a person who hasn't sinned egregiously love Jesus fully?
- Have you found yourself feeling like the woman felt toward Jesus?
- Did the woman receive forgiveness because she loved so much?
