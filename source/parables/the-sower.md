# The Sower

## Verses
- Mark 4:1-20
- Mathew 13:1-9
- Mathew 13:18-23
- Luke 8:4-8

## Discussion Questions
- Thoughts?
- Who was the crowd on the shore made up of?
- Who was Jesus referring to when he said "to those who are outside"?
- Is Jesus trying to make it difficult to understand what he's saying? Why?
- What does the seed represent? How about the soil?
- Do you relate to any of the groups Jesus describes?
- Can a person's heart change from one type of soil to another over time?
